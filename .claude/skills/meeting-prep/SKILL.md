# Meeting prep

Goal: walk into every external meeting knowing who we're talking to, what they
care about, and what we want out of the call.

## When

- An upcoming external meeting is detected — via a calendar reminder **email** or
  an **upcoming calendar event** (always scan both sources).
- Final prep and validation must happen **within the 3 days before the meeting**.

## Steps

1. **Qualify** — look up the account and attendees in the CRM (Attio): prospect?
   deal (which stage)? partner? Check the tasks on the account (next steps live
   there, per the Attio convention) and the notes attached to upcoming events.
2. **Gather transcripts** — fetch all recent transcripts and notes involving the
   account's people, from every source: Google Drive (Meet transcripts), Attio
   (call recordings, notes, emails), Linear (mentions in issues and documents).
   Read them.
3. **Extract** — derive the objectives for this call with this person, notably by
   recalling the **pains they have expressed** (short quotes when useful).
4. **New person?** — if the attendee appears in no transcript or CRM history, run
   a short background investigation on them (Google, LinkedIn): role, background,
   anything relevant to the conversation.
5. **Validate** — deliver a compact prep brief (account context, attendees, pains,
   objectives, proposed talking points) and get the user's final validation.
6. **Recap** — leave the user a written recap they can re-read right before the call.
